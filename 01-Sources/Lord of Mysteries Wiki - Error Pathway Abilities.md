## Extracted facts

- Sequence 9 Marauder Superior Observation senses valuable items within a 10-meter range, including concealed valuables inside drawers/cupboards, without revealing their exact contents or location; it can also detect a concealed target by sensing the valuables that target carries.
- Sequence 6 Prometheus Superior Observation extends to a 50-meter range with approximate value/type sensing, resistible only by Beyonder Concealment.
- Sequence 5 Dream Stealer's conceptualized Theft operates within an 80-meter range and can steal a Beyonder power for roughly half an hour of use; an unused stolen power can instead be retained for about a week.
- Sequence 4 Parasite can hold up to three stolen abilities from the same target (only one usable at a time), can steal at least 500 meters of Distance/Position, and stolen Beyonder powers remain usable for about two hours regardless of the original holder's status.
- Sequence 3 Mentor of Deceit can steal three Beyonder powers in a single attempt, prioritizing a target's three most recently used powers.
- Sequence 2 Trojan Horse of Destiny can steal up to six Beyonder powers from a single target in one attempt, each remaining usable for about a week.
- Sequence 0 Error can create at least two Sequence 1-equivalent Avatars; a single Avatar can theoretically approach true Sequence 1 strength but this is described as almost impossible and destabilizing, with a practical limit of not more than two such Avatars under characteristic conservation.
- Pathway exchange for Fool/Door/Error is only possible from Sequence 3 upward (noted on the corresponding Door Pathway overview page, applicable to all three Lord of Mysteries Pathways).

## Short quotations

- "They are be able to directly sense valuable items within a ten meter range, but can't determine where they exactly are." (Marauder, Superior Observation)
- "They can directly sense where valuable items are within a 50-meter range, their approximate value, and possible types." (Prometheus, Superior Observation)
- "They can Steal up to 6 Beyonder powers from a single target in only one attempt, and they will still be usable for a week after the activation." (Trojan Horse of Destiny)
