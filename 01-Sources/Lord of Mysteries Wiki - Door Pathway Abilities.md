## Extracted facts

- Sequence 6 Scribe can initially Record 1 demigod-level power, 8 powers at Sequence 5-6, and 20 powers at Sequence 7-9; after full potion digestion this expands to 2 demigod-level slots. Recorded power strength scales down more the higher the original Sequence (demigod-level halved at best; Sequence 5-6 keeps over 50%; Sequence 7 and lower is almost full strength).
- Sequence 5 Traveler can Record up to 4 demigod-level powers at close to true Sequence 4 strength.
- Sequence 4 Secrets Sorcerer can Record Angel-level powers (including King of Angels-level), though notably weaker than the original; Exile lasts only 20 seconds at this Sequence before the target automatically returns.
- Sequence 2 Planeswalker can miniaturize people/objects/scenes into a toy-like stored state covering a city the size of Backlund; affected beings must be released within 24 hours or become permanently converted, and can only store one miniaturized space at a time (swappable).
- Sequence 0 Door can create at least two Sequence 1 Avatars via the Error authority; Sealing a King of Angels bearing the Door Uniqueness plus three Sequence 1 Characteristics requires two True Gods acting together.
- Pathway exchange for Fool/Door/Error is only possible from Sequence 3 upward.

## Short quotations

- "They can Record up to 4 [Demigod] powers, whose effects are close to a true Sequence 4." (Traveler, Strengthened Record)
- "Secrets Sorcerers are now able to Record Angel-level Beyonder powers, even those of Kings of Angels; however, those Beyonder powers will be much weaker than the real deal." (Secrets Sorcerer)
