# Door and Sefirah Castle Access

## Native Door Scope

Door, Space, Sealing, Alternate Worlds, Position, Replication, and supporting Concealment belong to [[Door Pathway Authority Map]]. [[Spirit World Access (Door)]] is a travel mechanism, not complete Spirit World dominion.

## Conditional Access

A qualified Door holder controlling [[Sefirah Castle]] may access Fool or Error capabilities according to level and state of control. This belongs to the holder's Sefirah state; ordinary Door Beyonders do not inherit those Authorities.
