# Door and Sefirah Castle Access

## Native Door Authorities

- [[Door (Door Pathway)]], [[Space (Door)]], [[Sealing (Door)]], [[Alternate Worlds (Door)]], [[Position (Door)]], and [[Replication (Door)]].
- [[Spirit World Access (Door)]] is an operational power-domain used for travel, not Spirit World dominion.

## Conditional borrowing

A Door holder who controls [[Sefirah Castle]] can borrow Fool and Error abilities or Authorities according to level of control. This access is conditional; it is not automatic Pathway inheritance.

See [[Lord of Mysteries Group]] for complete group structure.
