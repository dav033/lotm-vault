# Error Pathway Symbolism

## Core Pathway Symbolism

- **Error / Bug** — manifestation of Errors within normal or supernatural systems.
- **Loophole in Rules** — exploitable incompleteness, inconsistency, or exception in a rule.

These are conceptual Symbolisms, not the Pathway's visual emblem.

## Sequence 0 Symbolic Expressions

- **Trojan Horse of Destiny** — infiltration and replacement through Fate or identity.
- **Worm that Erodes Time** — temporal erosion and Theft.
- **Manifestation of All Errors** — embodiment of Bugs and Loopholes.

These titles express the Pathway's concepts. They do not each establish a separate unrestricted Authority.
