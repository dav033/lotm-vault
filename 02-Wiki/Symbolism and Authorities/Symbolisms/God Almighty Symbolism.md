# God Almighty Symbolism

The Pillar integrates Omnipotence and Omniscience as high-order, non-absolute system states. No individual Pathway is confirmed to hold a separate all-encompassing Omnipotence Authority.

## Pathway contributions

- [[Spectator|Visionary]]: mind, discernment, dreams, envisioning.
- [[Bard|Sun]]: Beginning, light, holiness, energy, persistence, order.
- [[Sailor|Tyrant]]: Omnipotence, Maker, matter, forces, weather, calamity, tyranny.
- [[Reader|White Tower]]: Omniscience, principles, wisdom, analysis, partial creation.
- [[Secrets Supplicant|Hanged Man]]: Omnipotent, degeneration, grazing, adaptation, [[Sacrifice (Hanged Man)]], [[Sin-Bearing]].
