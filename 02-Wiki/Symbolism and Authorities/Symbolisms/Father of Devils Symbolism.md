# Father of Devils Symbolism

The group combines Abyss’s offensive descent through desire, degeneration, filth, and corruption with Chained’s Binding and Unity/Collection of dangerous states, objects, and Deviants.

## Explicit Chained Symbolisms

- Binding
- Unity / Collection

## Authority families

- Abyss: [[Desire]], [[Degeneration]], [[Filth]], [[Corruption]], [[Curse]], [[Deviants]].
- Chained: [[Binding]], [[Curse]], [[Deviants]], [[Objects]].

## Constraint

No shared [[Desire]] Authority is confirmed. [[Curse]] and [[Deviants]] are the explicit convergence points.
