# Demon of Knowledge Symbolism

[[Knowledge and Information]] is the GOO-level Symbolism: the ontological content, state, structure, and relations through which things are what they are. It is not merely “knowing everything.”

## Two Pathway expressions

- [[Mystery Pryer|Hermit]] makes mystical knowledge, secrets, symbols, and information into supernatural causality.
- [[Savant|Paragon]] turns reality-oriented knowledge into analysis, principles, manufacture, and civilization.

## Related Authorities

- Hermit: [[Mystical Knowledge]], [[Mystery Prying]], [[Hiding]], partial [[Education]], partial [[Spirit World Mentor]].
- Paragon: [[Reality Knowledge]], [[Principles]], [[Analysis]], [[Manufacture]], [[Civilization]], partial [[Education]], partial [[Spirit World Mentor]].

## Constraint

Technology, science, progress, machinery, and artifacts are applications or domains unless a primary source explicitly names them as Authorities.
