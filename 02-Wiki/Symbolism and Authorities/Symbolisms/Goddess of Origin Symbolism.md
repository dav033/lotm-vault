# Goddess of Origin Symbolism

The group combines Mother’s origin, gestation, environmental support, growth, death, and return with Moon’s lunar Spirituality, Beauty, Summoning, organism differentiation, and blood-mediated life.

## Pathway Symbolisms

- Mother of All: the maternal/origin Symbolism underlying [[Mother]] Authority.
- Moon: a Symbolism that gathers Spirituality, Beauty, Summoning, partial Life, partial Darkness, partial Bizarreness, and reproductive imagery.

## Constraint

Fertility and Reproduction are functions or images, not independently confirmed Authorities for these Pathways.
