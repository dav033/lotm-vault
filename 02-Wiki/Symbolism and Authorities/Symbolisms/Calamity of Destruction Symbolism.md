# Calamity of Destruction Symbolism

This Symbolism describes calamity as an engineered cosmic process: instability and contradiction can be collapsed into Chaos, while conflict can be organized, expanded, conquered, and deliberately destroyed. City of Calamity provides source-level unity, scale, and symbolic orchestration.

## Related Authorities

### Demoness contribution

- [[Chaos]]: Catastrophe, Apocalypse, and terminal convergence.
- [[Primordium]]: beginning, stillness, and petrification-related expression.
- [[Mirror World]]: reflected existence and distributed continuity.
- [[Charm]], [[Femininity]], [[Disease]], [[Plague]], [[Black Magic]].

### Red Priest contribution

- [[War]]: organized collective conflict.
- [[Conquest]]: stable incorporation of what is subdued.
- [[Destruction]]: direct terminal annihilation.
- [[Sacrifice]] and [[Masculinity]].

## GOO relationship

[[Calamity of Destruction]] is not merely a larger explosion. Its emergent GOO-scale operation is symbolic calamity designed and deployed across cosmic scale. This is a strong inference from the supplied dossier, not an exhaustive canonical Authority list.
