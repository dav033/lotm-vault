# Eternal Darkness Symbolism

[[Eternal Darkness]] represents silence, lasting darkness, death, eternal rest, twilight, decay, and time understood as a movement toward an ending.

## Pathway expressions

- [[Sleepless]]: silence, eternal darkness, horror, concealment, spirituality, and misfortune.
- [[Corpse Collector]]: death, pallor, undead existence, enslavement, and the endpoint.
- [[Warrior]]: twilight, decay, passage of time, holiness, combat, and proxy.
