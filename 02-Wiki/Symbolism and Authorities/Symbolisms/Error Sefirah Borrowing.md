# Error and Sefirah Castle Access

## Native Error Scope

Error, Theft, Deceit, Time jurisdiction, and partial Fate jurisdiction belong to [[Error Pathway Authority Map]]. Decryption and Parasitism remain Powers or mechanisms.

## Conditional Access

A qualified Error holder controlling [[Sefirah Castle]] may access Fool or Door capabilities according to level and state of control. This is conditional access, not native ownership by every Error Beyonder.
