# Distortion (Black Emperor)

Distortion is a subordinate [[Black Emperor Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority under [[Autocrat (Black Emperor)]]. It alters concepts, relationships, meanings, rules, or contractual clauses within limits rather than replacing every target completely.

## Counter-relation

The supplied source reports a mutual counter-relation with Notarization. See [[The Anarchy Group Counters]].

## Symbolic Derivation

Distortion inherits [[Shadow of Order]] through its immediate parent Authority, [[Autocrat (Black Emperor)]]. It is not derived from Disorder.
