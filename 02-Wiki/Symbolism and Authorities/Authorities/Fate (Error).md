# Fate (Error)

Partial Pathway jurisdiction over Fate. It supports sensing Fate's ripples and stealing a target's Fate, identity, or Destiny-related position at high Sequence.

This is distinct from [[Fate (Fool)]] and from the complete Pillar-level [[Beacon of Destiny]] Symbolism.
