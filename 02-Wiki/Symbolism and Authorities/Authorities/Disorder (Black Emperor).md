# Disorder (Black Emperor)

The author post names this Authority 混乱 (*hunluan*). The [[Black Emperor Pathway (disambiguation)]] possesses the larger, active share. This must not be conflated with 失序 (*shixu*) in the titles The Anarchy and Nation of Disorder, or merged with the fate-oriented [[Disorder (Wheel of Fortune)]] merely because both Authorities use 混乱.
