# Disorder (Justiciar)

The author post names this limited Authority share 混乱 (*hunluan*). The [[Justiciar Pathway (disambiguation)]] can detect, calm, restore, or remove Disorder. This must not be conflated with 失序 (*shixu*) in The Anarchy's titles or merged with [[Disorder (Wheel of Fortune)]] merely because both use 混乱.
