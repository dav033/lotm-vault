# Door Pathway Authority Map

## Native Authorities and Jurisdictions

- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] — passage, access, opening, closure, and Doors between realms.
- [[Space (Door)]] — spatial movement, division, joining, fragmentation, collapse, and higher-dimensional travel.
- [[Sealing (Door)]] — physical, spatial, and conceptual seals.
- [[Alternate Worlds (Door)]] — access to and creation of alternate worlds or special realms.
- [[Replication (Door)]] — recreates understood powers, scenes, or effects; Record is its restricted predecessor.
- [[Position (Door)]] — locating, hiding, fixing, or influencing Position.
- [[Concealment (Door)]] — secrets and concealed spaces; supporting jurisdiction.

## Derived Powers and States

- [[Spirit World Access (Door)]] — travel mechanism, not Spirit World dominion.
- Teleportation, Blink, and Wandering are Powers based on Door, Space, Position, and the relevant travel medium.
- Rapid Blink can produce an omnipresent state; that state is not an Authority.

## Sequence Expression

- [[Apprentice]] — Door Opening.
- [[Trickmaster]] — minor tricks and short-range Escape Trick.
- [[Astrologer]] — Astrology, Interference, stronger Door Opening.
- [[Scribe]] — Record.
- [[Traveler]] — Positioning, Teleportation, Blink.
- [[Secrets Sorcerer]] — Space Concealment, Secret Keeping, Exile, qualitative Record.
- [[Wanderer]] — Astral Wandering, Space Tearing, Spatial Cage.
- [[Planeswalker]] — Replication, Symbolization, realm Doors, conceptual Sealing.
- [[Key of Stars]] — Position Authority, Space Fragmentation, advanced Sealing.
- [[Door]] — complete Pathway Authorities and conceptual Door applications.

Detailed Authority-to-Power relations belong in each Sequence note.
