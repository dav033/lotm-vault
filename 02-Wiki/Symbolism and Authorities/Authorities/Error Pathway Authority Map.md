# Error Pathway Authority Map

## Native Authorities and Jurisdictions

- [[Error (Error Pathway)]] — Errors, Bugs, and Loopholes in rules or relationships.
- [[Theft (Error)]] — steals physical or abstract targets.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] — deceives minds, perception, rules, mysticism mechanisms, or self-state.
- [[Time (Error)]] — Pathway-specific temporal jurisdiction, primarily through Theft and Loopholes.
- [[Fate (Error)]] — partial jurisdiction over Fate, identity, and Destiny-related replacement.

## Derived Powers and Mechanisms

- Parasitism and Avatars are Pathway Powers using Worms of Time, Theft, identity, and mystical connections; they are not independent Authorities here.
- Decryption uses Spirit World information and enhanced deduction; it is not promoted to Authority.
- Dreams, memories, language, attacks, and abilities are targets of Theft, not separate native Authorities.

## Sequence Expression

- [[Marauder]] — physical Theft and observation.
- [[Swindler]] — Eloquence, Thought Misdirection, Mental Disruption.
- [[Cryptologist]] — Decryption.
- [[Prometheus]] — remote and Beyonder Power Theft.
- [[Dream Stealer]] — abstract Theft of thoughts, Dreams, memories, abilities, and attacks.
- [[Parasite]] — Parasitism, Avatars, Theft of Life and connections.
- [[Mentor of Deceit]] — initial Deceit over abstract concepts and rules.
- [[Trojan Horse of Destiny]] — Loopholes, partial Theft Authority, Fate and identity Theft.
- [[Worm of Time]] — Time, anchors, and Authority Theft.
- [[Error]] — complete Pathway Authorities and conceptual Errors.

Detailed Authority-to-Power relations belong in each Sequence note.
