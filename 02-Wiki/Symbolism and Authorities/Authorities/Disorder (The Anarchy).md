# Disorder (The Anarchy)

Authority over loss of coherence in choices, measures, relations, powers, thoughts, and rules. Black Emperor holds the major share and can disorder Order itself; Justiciar holds a limited share used to restore, calm, or eliminate Disorder. Distinct from [[Disorder]] in Wheel of Fortune.
