# Autocrat (Black Emperor)

Autocrat is a core [[Black Emperor Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority under [[Shadow of Order]]. It concerns unilateral control and exploitation of an ordered system; [[Distortion (Black Emperor)]] is a subordinate Authority rather than a synonym.

## Symbolic Derivation

The source directly places Autocrat under [[Shadow of Order]].
