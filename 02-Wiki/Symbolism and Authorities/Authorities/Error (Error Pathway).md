# Error (Error Pathway)

Authority over Errors, Bugs, and Loopholes in rules or relationships. It can create, exploit, redirect, or repair an Error when valid conditions exist.

This is one cornerstone of the integrated [[Change (Fool Symbolism)]] context of [[Lord of Mysteries]]; it is not identical to the whole Change Symbolism.

## Applications

- Find or create Loopholes in rules.
- Redirect an effect through an induced Error.
- Establish equivalence through similarity or a temporary accepted premise.
- Avoid an attack by separating the user from ordinary interaction with reality.
