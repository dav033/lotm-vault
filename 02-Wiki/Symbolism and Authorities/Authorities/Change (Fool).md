# Change (Fool)

This Pathway-scoped Authority covers Transformation and the normal-change expressions attributed to the [[Fool Pathway (disambiguation)]]. The official author-post source records Transformation in its completed Sequence 0 Fool Authority summary, while the supplied secondary research maps that material to Change.

This is not unrestricted ownership of [[Change (Lord of Mysteries)]], which remains a distinct group-level Symbolism. [[Grafting and Reassembly]] is also modeled as a separate Authority rather than as another name for Change.
