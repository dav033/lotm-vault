- [[Door Pathway Authority Map]]

Fool, Door, and Error are neighbouring Pathways inside one group. Their native Authorities remain distinct below the integrated [[Lord of Mysteries]] state.

## Ontology

```text
Symbolism
  governs or supports an Authority
    which produces or elevates a Power
      which appears at a specific Sequence
```

- **Explicit Powers** are named abilities available at a Sequence.
- **Authority → Power Mapping** records conceptual derivation and evidence strength.
- A Power does not become an Authority because it is versatile or visually dramatic.
- [[Sefirah Castle]] access can provide conditional cross-Pathway use; it does not rewrite native Pathway ownership.
