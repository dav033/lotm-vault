# Ontology and Linking Rules

## Canonical structure

```text
Pathway Group
├─ Sefirah
├─ Great Old One / Pillar
└─ Pathways
   ├─ Pathway Hub
   └─ Sequences 9–0
```

The graph is not a one-way inheritance tree. A Great Old One is the integrated state formed from its Sefirah and neighbouring Pathways. Each Pathway contributes its own Authorities and Symbolisms to that state.

## Conceptual model

```text
Symbolism → Authority → Power
Pathway → expresses Authority
Sequence → manifests Power
Sefirah + required Pathways → Great Old One
qualified holder + Sefirah control → conditional borrowed access
```

## Node types

- **Pathway Group**: navigation hub for one Sefirah, one Great Old One configuration, and all neighbouring Pathways.
- **Pathway**: complete Sequence 9–0 progression route. Never use its Sequence 9 note as the Pathway hub.
- **Sequence**: one level within a Pathway.
- **Sefirah**: integrating cosmic component associated with a neighbouring Pathway group.
- **Great Old One / Pillar**: integrated state requiring its Sefirah and the relevant Pathways.
- **Symbolism**: metaphysical concept represented at Pathway or group level. It is not a visual sigil.
- **Authority**: conceptual jurisdiction expressed by a Pathway or integrated state.
- **Power**: concrete ability derived from one or more Authorities or domains.
- **Conditional access map**: access borrowed through Sefirah control; never automatic inheritance by a Pathway.

## Required links

- Every Group Hub links its Sefirah, Great Old One, Pathway Hubs, and group knowledge maps.
- Every Pathway Hub links its Group Hub, Sefirah, Great Old One, Sequences, Symbolism map, and Authority map when available.
- Every Sequence links its Pathway Hub and Group Hub.
- Every Great Old One and Sefirah links the same Group Hub and Pathway Hubs.
- Every Symbolism links its level (`pathway` or `group`) and related Authorities.
- Every Authority links its bearer Pathway, supporting Symbolism when known, and concrete Powers.
- Every Power links its Sequence source when known and the Authority or domain from which it derives.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- Do not treat Sequence 9 as the entire Pathway.
- Do not model a Pathway as inheriting every Great Old One or Pillar Symbolism.
- Do not promote visual motifs, spell effects, or operational domains to Authorities without evidence.
- Do not treat Sefirah borrowing as native Pathway ownership.
- Do not place cross-group states such as [[Fourth Pillar]] inside one Great Old One folder.

## Evidence labels

- `explicit-canon`: directly stated in the novels or official material.
- `strong-inference`: strongly supported by repeated canonical mechanics.
- `probable-interpretation`: plausible synthesis with incomplete direct support.
- `speculation`: retained only as a clearly marked hypothesis.

Research dossiers are syntheses. Their cited primary or official references are verification targets. Community wikis and Reddit are secondary navigation or interpretation sources and must not outrank canon.
