# Eternal Darkness

Great Old One formed through [[River of Eternal Darkness]] and the Darkness, Death, and Twilight Giant Pathways.

Its complete nature is expressed through silence, eternal darkness, death, eternal rest, twilight, decay, and passage toward an ending. Its relation to the [[Fourth Pillar]] is a higher-level convergence with [[Calamity of Destruction]], not an extra Pathway in this group.
