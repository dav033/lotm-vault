# Father of Devils

Great Old One integrating [[Chained Pathway]] and [[Abyss Pathway]] through [[Tenebrous World]].

## Core distinction

[[Desire]] is an explicit Abyss Authority. [[Binding]] is a distinct Chained Authority that can restrain desire, madness, beings, objects, and supernatural tendencies. Do not model this group as a simple Desire-versus-Restraint symmetry.
