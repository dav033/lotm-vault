# God Almighty

Pillar integrating Visionary, Sun, Tyrant, White Tower, and Hanged Man through [[Chaos Sea]]. It is not merely five Sequence 0 entities added together.

## Pillar-level state

Omnipotence and Omniscience are system-relative Symbolisms/states, not logical absolutes. The complete integration supplies unified execution, knowledge, energy, matter, adaptation, and order.
