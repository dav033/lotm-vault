# Spirit World Access

Door's operational use of Spirit World correspondences for passage, navigation, Blink, and Teleportation.

This is a Power-domain and travel mechanism, not complete [[Dominator of the Spirit World]] ownership. At higher Sequences, Wandering expands into the Astral World, Cosmos, and higher-dimensional routes.
