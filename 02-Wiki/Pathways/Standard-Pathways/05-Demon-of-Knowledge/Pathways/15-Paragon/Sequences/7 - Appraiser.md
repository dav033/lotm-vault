# Sequence 7 - Appraiser

## Extracted Ability Data

**New Abilities**

- **Appraisal**: **Appraisers** are able to use this ability to rapid identify the powers and problems of most **Beyonder** items without **Divination** or reasoning.
	- This allows them to use those items while keeping the danger to the minimum.
		- If the target item contains a higher status than their **Sequence**, or they lack certain knowledge of by **Recall**, their **Appraisal** will be incomplete or misunderstood.
		- The **Appraiser** also has the ability to **Appraise** other things as well.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 7 Appraiser owns an Authority.

- [[Reality Knowledge (Paragon)]] → **Appraisal** — rapidly identifying an item's true, real properties without needing Divination or reasoning.
