# Sequence 9 - Assassin

## Explicit Powers

**Traits**

- **Heightened Senses**: They possess sharp ears and eagle-like vision.
	- **Night Vision**: **Assassins** can easily see through darkness.

**New Abilities**

- **Shadow Concealment**: **Assassins** possess the ability to **Conceal** themselves within darkness and **Shadows**, allowing them to hide from their targets.
	- Sprinting at full speed weakens this ability's effectiveness.

## Neighbouring Pathways
- [[Red Priest Pathway (disambiguation)|Red Priest Pathway]]

## Authority → Power Mapping

- No Authority is named at Sequence 9. **Shadow Concealment** is a general hiding technique, not a named Demoness Authority — Concealment belongs to the Darkness Pathway's own [[Concealment (Darkness)]], a different group entirely.
- **Heightened Senses** and **Night Vision** remain unattributed supporting traits.
