# Sequence 8 - Robot

## Extracted Ability Data

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: All aspects of their physical abilities have been significantly improved.
	- This makes **Robots** naturally talented in fighting and shooting.
- **Mental Enhancement**: They have powerful calculation skills and very precise control.
- They gain proficiency in **[Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art")** and **Anti-Divination** skills.
	- Even without laying eyes on [Ludwig](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwig_Phil "Ludwig Phil") or hearing his voice [Kolobo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kolobo "Kolobo") was able to sense his ominous aura, allowing him to detect danger he represented.
		- By just looking at [Sow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sow "Sow"), [Kolobo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kolobo "Kolobo") was able to detect that something bad would happen to one who would interact with him in near future. Later [Sow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sow "Sow") tried to kill [Camus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Camus_Castiya "Camus Castiya")

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 8 Robot owns an Authority.

- **Physical Enhancement** and **Mental Enhancement** remain baseline Beyonder trait improvements without a supported Authority assignment.
- The unnamed proficiency in Divination and Anti-Divination hints at the [[Fate (Wheel of Fortune)]] authority's **Foresight** domain, but the source gives it no standalone ability name to map directly.
