## Standard Structure

```text
Group Hub
└─ Pathways
   └─ Pathway Name
      ├─ Pathway Hub
      └─ Sequences
         └─ Sequence notes
```

Each Pathway hub links its group, Great Old One, Sefirah, Sequence notes, and available knowledge maps.
