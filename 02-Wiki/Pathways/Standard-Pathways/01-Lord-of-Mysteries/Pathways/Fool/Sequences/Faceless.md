# Faceless

## Explicit Powers

- **Shapeshifting** — changes facial features, voice, body shape, skin, hair, and scent within Pathway limits.
- **Identity Recall and Recognition** — accurately remembers appearances and temperaments and recognizes disguises.
- **Tracking and Anti-Tracking** — combines observation, acting, and bodily control to follow or evade targets.
- **Strengthened Previous Abilities** — improves Magician powers and prior physical control.

- [[Change (Fool)]] → **Shapeshifting** — strong inference from the author's Transformation/Change classification at Sequence 0.
