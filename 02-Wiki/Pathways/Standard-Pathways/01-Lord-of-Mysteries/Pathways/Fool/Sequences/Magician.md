# Magician

## Explicit Powers

- **Damage Transfer** — transfers an injury to a prepared substitute or another valid target.
- **Flaming Jump** — relocates through a nearby flame.
- **Air Bullet** — fires compressed air as a projectile.
- **Paper Figurine Substitute** — substitutes a paper figurine for the user when attacked or affected.
- **Flame Controlling** — manipulates nearby flames.
- **Underwater Breathing** — forms an invisible air conduit for breathing.
- **Bone Softening** — temporarily makes the body flexible enough to escape restraints.
- **Drawing Paper as Weapons** — hardens paper for combat use.
- **Illusion Creation** — creates visual, auditory, and olfactory illusions.
- **Minor Tricks** — performs small spell-like stage effects.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Reassembly (Fool)]] → **Damage Transfer**, **Underwater Breathing** — directly identified by the author as lower or specific applications of Reassembly.
- [[Fooling]] → **Paper Figurine Substitute**, **Illusion Creation** — strong mechanical interpretation; the sources do not label these low-Sequence abilities as direct Authority use.
- **Flaming Jump**, **Air Bullet**, **Flame Controlling**, **Bone Softening**, **Drawing Paper as Weapons**, and **Minor Tricks** remain abilities without a supported Authority assignment.
