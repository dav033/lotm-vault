# Clown

## Explicit Powers

- **Physical Enhancement** — improves strength, agility, and bodily resilience.
- **Body and Facial Expression Control** — precisely controls posture, muscles, and visible emotion.
- **Acrobatics, Coordination, and Balance** — grants exceptional physical control and movement.
- **Enhanced Danger Intuition** — provides short, unclear premonitions of immediate threats.
- **Paper Daggers** — temporarily hardens paper into sharp throwing or cutting weapons.
