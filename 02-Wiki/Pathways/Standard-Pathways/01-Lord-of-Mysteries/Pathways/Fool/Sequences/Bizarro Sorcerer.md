# Bizarro Sorcerer

## Explicit Powers

- **Worms of Spirit** — separates Mythical Creature components that can carry consciousness and powers.
- **Bestowal** — places Worms of Spirit in Marionettes so they can use selected powers.
- **Concealment** — conceals the user or related actions from ordinary detection.
- **Enhanced Marionette Control** — expands range, quantity, and control efficiency.
- **Marionette Interchange** — swaps position with a Marionette.
- **Enhanced Damage Transfer, Air Cannon, Flaming Jump, Illusions, Shapeshifting, and Spirit Body Threads** — strengthens earlier powers.
