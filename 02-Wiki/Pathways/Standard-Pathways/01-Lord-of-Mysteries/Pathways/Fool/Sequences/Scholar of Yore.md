# Scholar of Yore

## Explicit Powers

- **Historical Void Borrowing** — borrows power from the user's past state.
- **Historical Void Projection** — summons people or objects as Historical Projections.
- **Historical Void Hiding** — hides within the Historical Void under valid conditions.
- **Fog Creation and Temperature Reduction** — creates fog and lowers surrounding temperature.
- **Strengthened Previous Abilities** — improves substitution, damage transfer, movement, shapeshifting, Marionettes, and other inherited powers.
