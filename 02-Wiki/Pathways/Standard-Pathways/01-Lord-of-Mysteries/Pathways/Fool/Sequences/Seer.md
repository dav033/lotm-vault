# Seer

## Explicit Powers

- **Enhanced Memory** — improves retention of ritual and divination procedures.
- **Spirituality** — a modest increase in spiritual capacity.
- **Spirit Vision** — perceives spiritual conditions and related phenomena.
- **Divination Arts** — includes dream divination, dowsing, and other ritual methods.
- **Ritualistic Magic** — performs rituals supported by spirituality and mysticism knowledge.
- **Danger Intuition** — senses threats without identifying every detail.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 9 Seer owns an Authority.

- [[Fate (Fool)]] → **Divination Arts**, **Danger Intuition** — probable interpretation.
- [[Spirit World Dominion (Fool)]] → **Spirit Vision**, **Ritualistic Magic** — probable interpretation.
