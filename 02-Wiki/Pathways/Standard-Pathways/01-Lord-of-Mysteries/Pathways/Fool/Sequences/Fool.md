# Fool

## Explicit Powers

- **Fooling** — makes falsehood real, lowers cognition, produces conceptual change, and fools Time, History, Fate, or sequences of events.
- **Reassembly / Grafting** — recombines unrelated physical things or abstract concepts into an inconceivable but real result.
- **Controller of the Spirit World** — controls most Spirit World Authorities available to this Pathway and can command major Spirit World beings within limits.
- **Miracles** — grants god-level wishes and resets or extends earlier Miracle applications.
- **History** — manipulates historical states and Historical Void phenomena within Pathway limits.
- **Realm of Mysteries** — creates a rules-based Divine Kingdom with mystery and concealment effects.
- **Transformation** — performs high-level changes grounded in the Change symbolism.
- **Anchor of Destiny** — makes the Fool's words and existence difficult for Fate to bypass or alter.
- **Conceptual Marionettes** — applies Spirit Body Threads and Marionette control at conceptual scale.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Fooling]] → **Fooling Time**, **Fooling History**, **Fooling Fate**, cognition reduction, falsehood-to-reality effects.
- [[Reassembly (Fool)]] → **Reassembly / Grafting**, including conceptual combinations.
- [[Spirit World Dominion (Fool)]] → **Controller of the Spirit World**, Spirit Body Threads, command of Spirit World entities.
- [[Miracles]] → **Miracles**, Wish effects, and Resurrection-related restoration.
- [[History (Fool)]] → **History** and Historical Void applications.
- [[Concealment (Fool)]] → concealment within **Realm of Mysteries**.
- [[Change (Fool)]] → **Transformation** and normal conceptual change.
- [[Anchor of Destiny (Fool)]] → **Anchor of Destiny** effects.
- [[Puppetry (Fool)]] → **Conceptual Marionettes** — derived power-domain supported by Spirit World control, Change, and Bizarreness; not an independent Authority.
- [[Time (Fool)]] and [[Fate (Fool)]] describe limited jurisdictions exercised through Fooling or Anchor of Destiny, not unrestricted standalone ownership.
