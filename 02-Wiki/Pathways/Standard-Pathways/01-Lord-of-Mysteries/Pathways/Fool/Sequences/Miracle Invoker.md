# Miracle Invoker

## Explicit Powers

- **Wish Accumulation** — accumulates fulfilled minor wishes before granting greater ones.
- **Wish Granting** — produces Miracles within accumulated capacity and applicable limits.
- **Resurrection** — returns through the Historical Void; treated as a default Miracle with limited uses.
- **Jamming in Fate** — interferes with divination, prophecy, and the target's expected development.
- **Strengthened Historical Void Summoning** — summons stronger or more durable Historical Projections.
- **Enhanced Divination and Spirit-Body Travel** — strengthens earlier spiritual abilities.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Miracles]] → **Wish Accumulation**, **Wish Granting**, **Resurrection** — direct lower mastery of the Miracles Authority.
- [[History (Fool)]] → Historical Void mechanism used by **Resurrection** and improved summoning.
- [[Anchor of Destiny (Fool)]] and [[Fate (Fool)]] → **Jamming in Fate** — probable interpretation; the exact jurisdiction remains narrower than Sequence 0 Anchor of Destiny.
