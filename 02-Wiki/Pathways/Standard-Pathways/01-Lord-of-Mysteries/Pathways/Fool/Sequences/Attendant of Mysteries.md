# Attendant of Mysteries

## Explicit Powers

- **Spirit Body Threads Regeneration** — regenerates Spirit Body Threads associated with past targets and turns affected beings into Marionettes.
- **Reassembly / Tampering / Grafting** — combines physical entities, abstract concepts, directions, definitions, or relationships into a new real result.
- **Strengthened Previous Abilities** — raises earlier Fool Pathway powers to Angel level.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Reassembly (Fool)]] → **Reassembly / Tampering / Grafting** — direct initial mastery of this Authority.
- [[Spirit World Dominion (Fool)]] → **Spirit Body Threads Regeneration** — direct Spirit World component.
- [[Change (Fool)]] and [[Inconceivable and Bizarreness (Lord of Mysteries)]] → conceptual basis of Grafting and regenerated Marionette effects.
- [[Puppetry (Fool)]] remains the resulting derived power-domain, not an independent Authority.
