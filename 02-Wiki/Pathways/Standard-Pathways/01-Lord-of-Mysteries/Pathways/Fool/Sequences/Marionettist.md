# Marionettist

## Explicit Powers

- **Spirit Body Threads Perception** — perceives the threads associated with living and spiritual targets.
- **Spirit Body Threads Manipulation** — gains initial control and eventually converts a target into a Marionette.
- **Marionette Control** — controls Marionettes, shares their senses, and uses their original Beyonder powers.
- **Hidden-Entity Detection** — detects invisible or parasitic beings through abnormal thread patterns.
- **Strengthened Previous Abilities** — improves prior Fool Pathway powers.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Spirit World Dominion (Fool)]] → **Spirit Body Threads Perception and Manipulation** — directly supported as applications of Spirit World control.
- [[Puppetry (Fool)]] → **Marionette Control** — derived power-domain, not an independent Authority.
- [[Change (Fool)]] and [[Inconceivable and Bizarreness (Lord of Mysteries)]] contribute to Marionette conversion — author-described composite basis at higher level.
