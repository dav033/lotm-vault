# Scribe

## Explicit Powers

- **Record** — records an observed active Beyonder power and stores one later use.
- **Power-Slot Management** — capacity and success depend on the recorded power's level, familiarity, and potion digestion.
- **Strengthened Previous Abilities** — improves Astrology, Door Opening, and Trickmaster spells.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Replication (Door)]] → **Record** — directly established lower and restricted form of the later Replication Authority.
- [[Door (Door Pathway)]], [[Space (Door)]], and [[Position (Door)]] → strengthened inherited Door and Astrology powers.
- Slot limits are mechanics of **Record**, not a separate Authority.
