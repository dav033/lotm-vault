# Apprentice

## Explicit Powers

- **Door Opening** — opens symbolic incorporeal Doors to phase through ordinary walls and barriers.
- **Lock Opening** — opens mundane locks and some weak mystical locks.
- **Localized Opening** — creates an Opening on a selected body area or compatible target.
- **Ritualistic Magic** — performs rituals supported by the Pathway's spirituality.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 9 Apprentice owns an Authority.

- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] → **Door Opening**, **Lock Opening**, **Localized Opening** — direct lower expression of the Door concept.
- [[Space (Door)]] → phasing aspect of **Door Opening** — strong inference.
- [[Spirit World Access (Door)]] → ordinary wall passage mechanism — derived Power/mechanism, not an Authority.
- **Ritualistic Magic** has no independent Authority assignment.
