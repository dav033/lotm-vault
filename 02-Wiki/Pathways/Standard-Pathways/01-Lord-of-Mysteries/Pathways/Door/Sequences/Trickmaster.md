# Trickmaster

## Explicit Powers

- **Flash** — produces a burst that disrupts vision.
- **Black Screen** — creates a lightless curtain for concealment.
- **Gas Transfer** — moves gas between nearby locations.
- **Loud Noise** — creates a disruptive explosive sound.
- **Freezing** — chills by touch or short-range ray.
- **Electric Shock** — releases a limited electrical attack.
- **Fog**, **Wind**, and **Burning** — creates weak elemental or environmental effects.
- **Tumble** — produces rapid evasive movement.
- **Object Manipulation** — moves small objects remotely.
- **Escape Trick** — performs a short-range precursor to Blink.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] and [[Space (Door)]] → **Escape Trick** — lower spatial movement expression.
- [[Concealment (Door)]] → **Black Screen** — functional concealment, not proof of Authority mastery.
- Remaining tricks are explicit spells without supported independent Authority assignments.
