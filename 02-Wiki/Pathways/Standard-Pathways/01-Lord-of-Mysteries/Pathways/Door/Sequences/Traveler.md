# Traveler

## Explicit Powers

- **Positioning** — identifies the user's location and establishes a destination.
- **Traveler's Door / Teleportation** — travels through the Spirit World to a chosen position.
- **Blink** — performs repeated short-range teleportation.
- **Invisible Hand** — manipulates objects from a distance.
- **Strengthened Record** — stores stronger powers, including limited demigod-level examples.
