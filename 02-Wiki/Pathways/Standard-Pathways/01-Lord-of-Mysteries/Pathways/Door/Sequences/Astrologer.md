# Astrologer

## Explicit Powers

- **Astrology** — divines information and future tendencies through stars and a crystal ball.
- **Spiritual Intuition** — senses danger and some supernatural interference.
- **Interference** — disrupts another person's divination or spiritual intuition.
- **Memory Recall and Identity Comparison** — recalls details and detects inconsistencies in identity.
- **Strengthened Door Opening** — carries other people and creates small viewing Doors.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Position (Door)]] → locating and directional aspects of **Astrology** — strong inference.
- [[Concealment (Door)]] → **Interference** against divination — probable interpretation.
- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] and [[Space (Door)]] → **Strengthened Door Opening**.
- **Spiritual Intuition** and **Memory Recall** remain abilities without independent Authority status.
