# Key of Stars

## Explicit Powers

- **Position Authority** — locates self, targets, or objects and can hide or seal a Position.
- **Space Fragmentation** — splits, collapses, or degrades spatial regions.
- **Gravity Manipulation** — alters gravity through advanced control of space.
- **Advanced Sealing** — seals physical entities, concepts, rules, possibilities, convergence, or selected capabilities within limits.
- **Higher-Dimensional Teleportation** — travels through higher-dimensional spatial routes.
- **Enhanced Dimension Sight** — reads complex spatial and dimensional relationships.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Position (Door)]] → **Position Authority**, target location, and hidden Position.
- [[Space (Door)]] → **Space Fragmentation**, **Gravity Manipulation**, **Higher-Dimensional Teleportation**, **Dimension Sight**.
- [[Sealing (Door)]] → **Advanced Sealing** and sealing of Position.
- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] → higher-dimensional access and travel.
- [[Concealment (Door)]] → hiding a Position — supporting Authority, not the primary one.
