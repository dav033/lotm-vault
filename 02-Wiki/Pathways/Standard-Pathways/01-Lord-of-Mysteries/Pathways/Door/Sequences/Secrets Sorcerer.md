# Secrets Sorcerer

## Explicit Powers

- **Secret Keeping** — blocks sensing and tracking or turns the user into a mystical secret.
- **Space Concealment** — splits and conceals part of a spatial area.
- **Space Pocket** — creates a concealed space accessible through a designated Door.
- **Door Transformation** — transforms into illusory Doors and places the true body behind them.
- **Exile** — sends a controlled target into temporary space-time turbulence.
- **Enhanced Blink** — rapidly alternates positions and casts through separate Worms of Star.
- **Qualitative Record** — records stronger powers and supernatural states.
- **Restriction Opening** — uses Door Opening against some teleportation prohibitions.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Concealment (Door)]] → **Secret Keeping**, **Space Concealment**, **Space Pocket**.
- [[Space (Door)]] → **Space Concealment**, **Door Transformation**, **Exile**, **Enhanced Blink**.
- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] → **Door Transformation**, **Restriction Opening**, spatial entrances and exits.
- [[Replication (Door)]] → **Qualitative Record**.
- [[Sealing (Door)]] → restraining use of concealed space — early functional expression, not full Authority mastery.
