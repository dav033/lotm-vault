# Clown

## Traits

- **Physical Enhancement** — grants exceptional dexterity and speed, a moderate increase in strength, and near-complete control of bodily functions.
- **Body and Facial Expression Control** — allows deliberate control of posture, limbs, muscles, and visible emotion. This makes ordinary behavioral reading, including a Spectator's observation, unreliable; extreme shock or pain can still expose genuine reactions.
- **Acrobatics, Coordination, and Balance** — supports difficult climbing, evasive maneuvers, tightrope-like movement, and combat techniques. Balance is nearly inhuman but can still fail under extreme circumstances.
- **Spirituality** — receives a marginal increase.

## Explicit Powers

- **Enhanced Danger Intuition** — provides short-term but ambiguous premonitions. It can reveal people behind a closed door without Spirit Vision and can occasionally anticipate another person's next action; this prediction is infrequent and is most reliable when the action would harm the Clown.
- **Paper Daggers** — temporarily makes sheets of paper as hard and sharp as steel for use as thrown daggers or knives; they can lodge in solid stone and pierce flesh and bone.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- Fate, as a partial jurisdiction candidate → **Enhanced Danger Intuition** — probable interpretation, not native Authority ownership.
- [[Change (Lord of Mysteries)]] → **Body and Facial Expression Control** — weak group-symbolism precursor; not established as an Authority application or native Pathway ownership.
- **Physical Enhancement**, **Acrobatics**, and **Paper Daggers** remain explicit abilities without a supported Authority assignment.
