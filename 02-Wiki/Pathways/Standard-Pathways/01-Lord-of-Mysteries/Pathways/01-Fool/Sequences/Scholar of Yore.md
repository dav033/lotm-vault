# Scholar of Yore

## Explicit Powers

- **Historical Void Borrowing**: Borrows a past state or strength from the user's former self as often as Spirituality permits. At 80% digestion, the available Spirituality can support nearly ten five-minute borrowings without recovery.
- **Historical Void Projection**: Summons people, objects, or Marionettes from known history for a limited time.
  - Success and duration depend on knowledge of the relevant history, the target's level relative to the Scholar, and affinity with the target. A projection above the Scholar's level has only part of the original's strength.
  - At 80% digestion, an ordinary object or something formerly owned by the Scholar can remain for at most 15 minutes.
  - No more than three projections can exist at once, including those summoned by Marionettes. Projections of projections are easier to summon but weaker.
  - Projections normally lack intelligence and their counterpart's past memories, so they cannot answer questions unknown to the Scholar. A living target hidden in the Historical Void or a Concealed state can send their mind into their own projection.
  - A projection has Spirit Body Threads and can become a Marionette. Its duration remains vulnerable to Fate and Time effects.
  - A Uniqueness, or a person who accommodated one, cannot be summoned directly; summoning an object or person through which the Uniqueness can exert power can bypass part of this restriction.
- **Historical Void Hiding**: Enters gaps in the Historical Void known to the Scholar for as long as Spirituality lasts.
  - The Scholar must return to the same physical location on leaving. Borrowing or summoning inside a gap requires a past self to exist in that period.
  - The Scholar can send consciousness into a projection while hidden. Contact with an unlit point in the Fog of History is dangerous, while isolated history remains accessible if the Scholar knows it.
- **Fog Creation and Temperature Reduction**: Produces fog and lowers an area's temperature.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Paper Figurine Substitute**: Disrupts divination, prophecy, tracking, and gazes, and can temporarily substitute a target's body part or organ until the falsehood is discovered.
- **Damage Transfer**: Transfers part of an ailment, curse, prophecy, attack, or gaze to a paper figurine.
- **Flaming Jump**: Reaches 5 kilometres and can move objects to the Scholar or a Marionette.
- **Air Cannon**: Reaches adjustable coastal-defense-artillery force.
- **Shapeshifting**: Assumes creatures within a size limit, though not every copied organ functions; flesh can also be compressed into an object as small as a bookmark.
- **Spirit Body Threads Manipulation**: Initial control takes about 2 seconds and full control about 10 seconds. Conversion begins within 500 metres, while Marionette control and Interchange reach 5 kilometres.

## Mythical Creature Form

- The divine patterns on the Worms of Spirit become more complex and more expressive of Fool Pathway themes, while retaining individual variation. A Scholar can split about 600 worms.
- After complete digestion, the Scholar can recover after nearly 500 worms die at once. Lost corresponding characteristics can be replenished by absorbing Fool Pathway Beyonder Characteristics.
- Separate Worms of Spirit can instinctively listen to prayers within a limited range, answer simple ones, and pass complicated prayers to the main body.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[History (Fool)]] → **Historical Void Borrowing**, **Historical Void Projection**, and **Historical Void Hiding**. The Authority is reported as a combined expression of the group-level [[King of Space-Time]] and [[Inconceivable and Bizarreness (Lord of Mysteries)]] symbolisms.
- Concealment (Fool, partial/derived) → the concealment aspect of **Historical Void Hiding**: a secondary contribution, not the power's primary source.
- **Fog Creation** and **Temperature Reduction** remain explicit abilities without an established Authority assignment.
