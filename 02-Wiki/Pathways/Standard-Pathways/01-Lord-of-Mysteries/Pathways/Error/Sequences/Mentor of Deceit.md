# Mentor of Deceit

## Explicit Powers

- **Deceit** — deceives abstract concepts, supernatural perception, or rules within current limits.
- **Weakness and Rule Observation** — detects weaknesses and changes in Order or rules.
- **Enhanced Theft** — can steal several candidate powers with improved targeting.
- **Theft of Heavenly Secrets** — steals blessings, prayer responses, or concealed mystical opportunities under conditions.
- **Mastered Avatars** — creates and controls Avatars with greater independence.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Deceit (Error)]] → **Deceit**, supernatural misdirection, and exploitation of observed weaknesses.
- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Enhanced Theft**, **Theft of Heavenly Secrets**.
- [[Error (Error Pathway)]] → sensing rule changes and preparing exploitable Loopholes — precursor, not yet full Sequence 0 mastery.
- **Avatars** remain a Pathway Power, not an independent Authority.
