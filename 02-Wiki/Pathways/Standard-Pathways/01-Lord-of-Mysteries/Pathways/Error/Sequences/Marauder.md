# Marauder

## Explicit Powers

- **Theft / Stealing** — steals a physical item from a target without ordinary contact or detection.
- **Agile Hands** — improves wrist and finger speed, stability, and dexterity.
- **Superior Observation** — senses valuable objects within a limited range.
- **Physical Enhancement** — provides a modest physical improvement.
- **Short-Weapon Proficiency** — improves use of daggers, short swords, and similar weapons.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 9 Marauder owns an Authority.

- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Theft / Stealing** — low-Sequence Power on the conceptual line that later becomes Authority.
- **Agile Hands**, **Superior Observation**, **Physical Enhancement**, and **Short-Weapon Proficiency** remain traits without independent Authority status.
