# Worm of Time

## Explicit Powers

- **Time Theft** — steals Time, lifespan, temporal duration, or opportunities from a target.
- **Anchor Theft** — steals the anchors supporting another high-level existence.
- **Authority Theft** — temporarily steals an Authority or part of its usable effect under applicable limits.
- **Symbolism Decryption** — decrypts higher-level symbols and mystical structures.
- **Avatar Succession** — an Avatar can replace the main body by stealing Fate, identity, anchors, Spirit Body, consciousness, and psyche.
- **Temporal Loopholes** — exploits gaps in time limits, duration, or sequence of events.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Time Theft**, **Anchor Theft**, **Authority Theft**, and **Avatar Succession**.
- [[Time (Error)]] → temporal target and effect of **Time Theft** and **Temporal Loopholes**.
- [[Fate (Error)]] → Fate and identity components of **Avatar Succession**.
- [[Error (Error Pathway)]] → **Temporal Loopholes**.
- **Symbolism Decryption** remains a strengthened Power, not an independent Authority.
