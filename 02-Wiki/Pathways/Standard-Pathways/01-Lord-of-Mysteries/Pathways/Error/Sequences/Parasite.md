# Parasite

## Explicit Powers

- **Parasitism** — enters a host in Initial or Deep-Level form to influence or control it.
- **Worms of Time** — separates Mythical Creature components used for Parasitism and Avatars.
- **Avatars** — creates independent extensions that share identity and power with the main body.
- **Synchronization** — synchronizes information, state, or actions across Avatars.
- **Theft of Life** — steals lifespan or vital duration.
- **Expanded Conceptual Theft** — steals position, distance, powers, aspects of life, or links between a clone and its main body.
- **Enhanced Decryption and Observation** — improves detection of clues and targets.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Theft of Life**, stolen position/distance/powers, and stolen mystical connections.
- [[Time (Error)]] → temporal aspect of **Theft of Life** and **Worms of Time** — early jurisdictional expression.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → concealed replacement and host misdirection within **Parasitism**.
- **Parasitism**, **Avatars**, and **Synchronization** are Powers or operational domains, not independent Authorities in this map.
