# Cryptologist

## Explicit Powers

- **Decryption** — reconstructs mysticism-related truth or predicts developments from sufficient clues.
- **Enhanced Observation, Association, and Deduction** — detects subtle evidence and connects it logically.
- **Enhanced Spiritual Intuition** — senses relevant supernatural information and danger.
- **Mysticism Knowledge** — gains knowledge needed to interpret supernatural evidence.
- **Secret and Incantation Decryption** — deciphers dreams, illusions, locations, charms, Mystical Items, and Sealed Artifact clues.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Decryption** is an explicit Power that uses information from the Spirit World; current official evidence does not establish Decryption as an independent Authority.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → adversarial use of **Decryption** to detect deception — operational relationship, not ownership of a new Authority.
- Observation, deduction, intuition, and knowledge remain supporting abilities.
