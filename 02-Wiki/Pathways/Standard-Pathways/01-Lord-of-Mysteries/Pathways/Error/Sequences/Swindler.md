# Swindler

## Explicit Powers

- **Charm** — increases affinity and makes trust easier to establish.
- **Eloquence** — improves speech and adds a persuasive Beyonder effect.
- **Thought Misdirection** — misleads thought through speech, posture, action, or arranged context.
- **Mental Disruption** — induces limited hallucinations or cognitive interference.
- **Enhanced Superior Observation** — reads micro-expressions and small movements.
- **Strengthened Theft and Agility** — improves stealing technique and physical mobility.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Deceit (Error)]] → **Eloquence**, **Thought Misdirection**, **Mental Disruption**, supernatural use of **Charm** — lower Powers related to Deceit.
- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Strengthened Theft**.
- **Superior Observation** and physical agility remain supporting traits, not Authorities.
