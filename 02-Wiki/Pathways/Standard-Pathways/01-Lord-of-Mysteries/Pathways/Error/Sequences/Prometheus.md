# Prometheus

## Explicit Powers

- **Beyonder Power Theft** — temporarily steals an active or available Beyonder power for later use.
- **Remote Object Theft** — steals physical objects across a distance.
- **Conceptualized Theft** — expands Theft beyond direct sleight of hand.
- **Physical Enhancement** — improves the body beyond earlier Sequences.
- **Mental Resistance** — increases resistance to mental influence and control.
- **Strengthened Previous Abilities** — improves observation, Decryption, and deception-related powers.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Beyonder Power Theft**, **Remote Object Theft**, **Conceptualized Theft** — developing Power expression of the future Authority.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → strengthened deception-related abilities.
- **Physical Enhancement** and **Mental Resistance** have no independent Authority assignment.
