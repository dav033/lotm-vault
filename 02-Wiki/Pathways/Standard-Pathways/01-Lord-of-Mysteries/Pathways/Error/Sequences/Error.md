# Error

## Explicit Powers

- **Error / Bug / Loopholes** — creates, exploits, redirects, or repairs Errors in normal and supernatural rules.
- **Deceit** — deceives cognition, rules, mystical mechanisms, the self, or higher-level interactions.
- **Theft** — steals physical or abstract targets, including powers, identity, Fate, Time, anchors, connections, and Authorities.
- **Fate Manipulation through Theft** — senses Fate's ripples and replaces or steals Destiny-related relationships.
- **Time Manipulation through Theft** — steals duration, lifespan, timing, or temporal opportunities.
- **Conceptual Parasitism and Avatars** — uses Spirit Body Threads, identity, and stolen connections at conceptual level.
- **Rules-Based Divine Kingdom** — establishes an erroneous environment governed by exploitable rules.
- **Advanced Decryption** — extracts secrets and exploitable Loopholes from minimal information.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Error (Error Pathway)]] → **Error**, **Bug**, **Loopholes**, rule equivalence, redirection, and exploit creation.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → **Deceit** against minds, rules, self-state, and mystical systems.
- [[Theft (Error)]] → all concrete and abstract **Theft** applications.
- [[Fate (Error)]] → Fate sensing, Fate Theft, Destiny replacement.
- [[Time (Error)]] → Time Theft and temporal Loopholes.
- **Parasitism**, **Avatars**, and **Decryption** remain Powers or operating methods; they are not promoted to independent Authorities here.
