# Trojan Horse of Destiny

## Explicit Powers

- **Loopholes** — detects and exploits gaps in rules, conditions, identities, or connections.
- **Fate Theft** — steals a target's Fate or Destiny-related position.
- **Identity and Self-Awareness Theft** — steals identity, consciousness-related ownership, or self-recognition.
- **Beyonder Characteristic Theft** — steals Characteristics under difficult conditions.
- **Mystical Connection Theft** — severs and steals connections to external powers or anchors.
- **Spirit Body Threads Theft** — steals threads and redirects manipulation through Deceit.
- **Enhanced Parasitism** — parasitizes targets or living Sealed Artifacts more effectively.
- **Angel Avatars** — creates Avatars capable of Angel-level action.
- **Rule Deceit** — misdirects mysticism laws or rules.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Error (Error Pathway)]] → **Loopholes** and exploitation of rules or conditions.
- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Fate**, **Identity**, **Self-Awareness**, **Characteristic**, **Connection**, and **Spirit Body Threads Theft**.
- [[Fate (Error)]] → **Fate Theft** and Destiny-related replacement.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → **Rule Deceit** and redirection of stolen Spirit Body Threads.
- **Parasitism** and **Avatars** remain Powers, not separate Authorities.
