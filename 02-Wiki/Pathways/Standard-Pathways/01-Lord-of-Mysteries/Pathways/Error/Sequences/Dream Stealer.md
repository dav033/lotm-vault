# Dream Stealer

## Explicit Powers

- **Dream Theft** — steals, stores, transfers, or influences Dreams.
- **Thought and Intention Theft** — steals active thoughts, intentions, or decisions.
- **Memory, Knowledge, and Ideal Theft** — steals mental information or convictions.
- **General Ability Theft** — steals learned abilities, movement, speech, or language.
- **Attack Theft** — steals an incoming attack or its opportunity to occur.
- **Enhanced Beyonder Power Theft** — steals stronger supernatural powers more reliably.
- **Thought Usurpation** — replaces or redirects a target's intended thought or action.
- **Disguise / Theft of Heavenly Secrets** — hides information and steals prayer responses or related mystical opportunities.
- **Enhanced Decryption** — improves analysis of supernatural clues.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Theft (Error)]] → all forms of **Dream**, **Thought**, **Memory**, **Knowledge**, **Ability**, **Attack**, and **Heavenly Secret Theft**.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → **Thought Usurpation** and deceptive use of stolen information.
- Dreams are a target category of Theft here, not a separate native Authority.
- **Decryption** remains an explicit Power using Spirit World information.
