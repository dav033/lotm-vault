# Sequence 2 - Death Consul

## Explicit Powers

**Authorities**

- **Death**: "They" wield partial authority over **Death**.
	- **Absolute Death**: "They" can strip all vitality from an area. Before "Them", most **Resurrection** or **Substitution** abilities become ineffective.
		- A [**Miracle Invoker's**](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Miracle_Invoker "Fool Pathway/Abilities") **Resurrection** that involves **History** and **Change** is an exception.
		- **Nation of the Dead**: "They" can create a large city-sized domain filled with **Death** and **Pallor** where life not strong enough will instantly die and transform into **Undead** creatures.
		- Even beings with **Godhood** will begin to slowly **Wither** and **Perish**.
		- **Dead Death Consuls** are still **Death Consuls**. The boundary between **Life** and **Death** no longer applying to "Them", making "Them" extremely hard to kill.
		- Only by relying on special powers and methods can a **Death Consul** are be completely erased and reverted back into [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics").
				- This doesn't mean "They" are immortal as "Their" consciousness would gradually erode, becoming an eternally living walking corpse.
		- "They" can manipulate the objects produced by the corruption from the power at [the source of the **River Styx**](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness").
		- The spring water of the [Samaritan Women's Spring](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underground_Trier#Samaritan_Women's_Spring "Underground Trier") has multiple effects including repairing **Souls**, inducing **Sleep**, bringing complete **Death** or true **Dreams**, and granting lucidity in **Dreams**. "They" can make it manifest only one quality and enhance that effect.
			- [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers") made it so that anyone who touches the spring water will fall into a long **Sleep**, "Himself" included.
				- It has a slight effect on **True Gods**, but not much, unless that deity is already very weak or in a state of slumber.
								- Reversing its effects requires more spring water processed in a similar way but with the opposite effect or help from a **True God** with the corresponding authority.
- **Enslavement**: **Death Consuls** are capable of issuing commands that **Beyonders** below **Sequence 2** of the are nearly incapable of resisting.
	- Even **Undead** creatures of the [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") level—including [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") —are extraordinarily vulnerable when facing "Them".
- **Undead Judgment**: "They" do more damage against the **Undead** and have a suppression effect based on status, this applies to all **Undead**.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **King of the Dead**: A **Death Consul** is a **King of the Dead**, who is capable of housing a vast **Undead** army within "Their" own **Internal Underworld**.
	- At the same time, "They" can find and directly summon helpers from the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld").
- Other **Beyonder** powers from previous **Sequences** have been enhanced.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "They" become a complete [Mythical Creature](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is a **Feathered Serpent** ([Quetzalcōātl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetzalc%C5%8D%C4%81tl "wikipedia:Quetzalcōātl")), a serpent that is illusory and real. "Their" entire body is covered with huge dark-green scales while white feathers stained with pale yellow oil grow out from the gaps. On each feather, illusory black tubes extend outward, and each scale and feather bears strange symbols of different shapes.
	- Just the mere sight of "Them" would cause one's flesh to decompose, turning all those who look at "Them" into **Zombies** and various other **Undead** creatures.
		- A phantom image of "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") can give the sensation of rapid death, a type of damage that **Mirror Substitution** couldn't transfer.
		- In "Their" **Mythical Creature Form**, "They" are capable of spatting out a **Pale-White Flame** that is able to cover an entire mausoleum.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source explicitly names both Authorities at this Sequence: **Death** (Absolute Death, Nation of the Dead) → [[Death (Authority)]], with Nation of the Dead's domain of "**Death** and **Pallor**" also touching [[Pallor (Death)]]; **Enslavement** → [[Enslavement (Death Authority)]].
- **Undead Judgment** (bonus damage and suppression against all Undead) → [[Undead (Death)]]. The complete Mythical Creature Form is a general trait, not an Authority.
