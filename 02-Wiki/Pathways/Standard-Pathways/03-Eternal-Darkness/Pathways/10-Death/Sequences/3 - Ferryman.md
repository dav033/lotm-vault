# Sequence 3 - Ferryman

## Explicit Powers

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: Most forms of attack, aside from **Purification** (like that of the [Tyrant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway "Tyrant Pathway") or [Sun Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway "Sun Pathway")), are ineffective against the **Ferryman**, as though no one can kill a dead person.

**Authorities**

- **Death**: They wield partial authority over **Death**.
	- Any living being that makes eye contact with the **Ferryman** will die instantly; even entities with **Godhood** will suffer devastating damage.
		- Even without direct eye contact, those caught by the **Ferryman's** gaze will slowly **Wither** away, as if being ferried across the river of **Death** inch by inch.
		- From here on, they will become a part of the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") and **Death** itself.
		- Near the **River Styx** or its tributaries, **Ferrymen** can effectively utilize the power of those rivers.
				- They can float on the **River Styx** without sinking. Even when facing the true [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") or its tributaries, they can remain afloat for a very short time— *two seconds* at their current stage.
		- The **Ferryman** traverses back and forth between **Life** and **Death.**
		- Those touched by their left hand will almost irreversibly approach **Death**.
				- Those touched by their right hand will reverse the process of **Death**, regaining **Life.**

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Undying**: Instead of dying, **Reviving**, and losing all their memories, they would instead gradually transform into a sentient **Undead** creature over time.
	- This also means that they will not instantly die in the **Underworld**.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source explicitly names the Authority at this Sequence: **Death** ("'They' wield partial authority over Death") → [[Death (Authority)]].
- Strengthened **Undying** anticipates [[Undead (Death)]]. The Mythical Creature Form's prayer-response is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.
