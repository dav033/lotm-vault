# Sequence 4 - Undying

## Explicit Powers

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: Physical attacks are no longer fatal unless their body is completely destroyed.
- **Spirit Body**: An **Undying** is able to exist in a state that is between a physical body and a **Spirit Body**.

**New Abilities**

- **Reincarnation**: They possess a long lifespan; however, every *60 years*, they will die and then **Resurrect**, forgetting all their memories and resetting their madness levels. From there, they must then slowly retrieve their memories, as if starting a new life.
	- This is not completely without risk, the **Undying** would leave behind a death imprint in the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") with each **Death**. Once the number of **Deaths** reaches a certain level, their true bodies might be attracted to this [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") one day, causing them to truly die.
		- Dying too many times leads to an **Undying's** death imprints being washed away from the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness"), making their memories impossible to recover; their intelligence and rationality will degrade over time, eventually turning into wandering **Undead** monsters.
- **Underworld Authority**: They are more closely connected to the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") and can directly utilize its power.
	- **Sealing**: They can use the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") to **Seal** objects. This includes [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts"), allowing them to reduce their negative effects.
		- Initially, [Creeping Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creeping_Hunger "Creeping Hunger") needed to be fed a human’s soul and flesh every day. After [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers") **Sealed** it, it only needed to be fed every time it was used.
				- After fusing with [Mr. A](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mr._A "Mr. A"), [Creeping Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creeping_Hunger "Creeping Hunger") lost its **Seal**, gained living characteristics, and periodically praised the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator"). After [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers") **Sealed** it once more, it reverted to how it was before, though it would praise the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") if told to do so.
		- **Spirit World Traversal**: They can use their connection to the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") as an anchor to gain a certain degree of **Spirit World Traversal**.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Door to the Underworld**: Directly uses the power of the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") to create a combat environment favorable to themselves.
	- This battlefield will inflict enemies with the attraction, summoning, and ravings from within the **Underworld**.
		- Additionally, if lucky or well-connected in the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"), they could summon dangerous **Undead** creatures into reality.
		- This includes **Styx Servants**, which are equivalent to **Ferrymen**.
- **Language of the Dead**: At this stage, the **Language of the Dead** will evolve into a **Spell** system related to **Death**, **Spirits**, **Zombies**, **Withering**, and **Enslavement**.
	- **Withering Wind**: It brings about a gust of **Cold** wind that silently causes **Withering**.
		- [Patrick Bryan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Patrick_Bryan "Patrick Bryan") created a cold gust of **Withering Wind** that caused a glass window to quickly crumble into countless "snowflakes".

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Sequence 4 Demigods** they receive some level of **Godhood**, and with it comes an incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). Disregarding all consequences, they can release their incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") to become an incomplete **Quetzalcoatl**, also known as a **Feathered Serpent**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Reincarnation**'s death imprint in the [[River of Eternal Darkness]] and **Underworld Authority** (Sealing, Spirit World Traversal) anticipate [[Endpoint (Death)]].
- Strengthened **Language of the Dead** explicitly evolves into a spell system spanning "Death, Spirits, Zombies, Withering, and Enslavement" → [[Pallor (Death)]] (Withering) and [[Enslavement (Death Authority)]] jointly; eventual degradation into wandering Undead → [[Undead (Death)]]. The incomplete Mythical Creature Form is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.
