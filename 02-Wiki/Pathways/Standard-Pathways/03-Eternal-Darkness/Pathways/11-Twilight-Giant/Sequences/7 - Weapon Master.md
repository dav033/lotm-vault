# Sequence 7 - Weapon Master

## Explicit Powers

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: They possess a superhuman strength, physique, and agility.

**New Abilities**

- **Weapon Mastery**: With their **Physical Enhancement**, as long as it is a **Weapon**, once it is in their hands, they can use it at a master-level standard.
	- This includes **Beyonder Weapons**, [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item"), and [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") in the form of swords, guns, etc. and they can effectively reduce the damage caused by said items negative effects.
