# Sequence 8 - Midnight Poet

## Explicit Powers

Oh, the threat of horror, the hope of crimson cries!
One thing at least is certain—that this Life flies;
One thing is certain, and the rest is Lies;
The Flower that once has bloomed forever dies.

— *Lord of Mysteries*, Chapter 28

**New Abilities**

- **Midnight Poem**: **Midnight Poets** can cast effects through the recital of a poem related to the night or the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess"). These **Midnight Poems** can manifest all sorts of varying effects.
- **Midnight Poets** also have expertise in combat, marksmanship, climbing and spiritual perception.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Nocturnality**: The degree of enhancement has been strengthened.
	- Even at the tail end of a night, after six in the morning when the sun is beginning to rise, they will be strong enough to break a doors lock and the surrounding wood with a single punch.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Midnight Poem** remains a general technique tied to the Evernight Goddess, not a named Authority. Strengthened **Nocturnality** continues to anticipate [[Darkness (Authority)]].
