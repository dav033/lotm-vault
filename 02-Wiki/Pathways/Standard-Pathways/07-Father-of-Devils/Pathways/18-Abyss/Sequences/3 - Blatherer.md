# Sequence 3 - Blatherer

## Extracted Ability Data

**Authorities **

- **Corruption**: A **Blatherer** has gained a certain degree of the **Corruption** authority, which is primarily concentrated in the realms of the **Mind** and **Spirit**.
	- Their voice and each and every word they utter can cause **Beyonders** to either lose control, suffer severe damage, experience mental confusion, or be hexed with all sorts of **Curses**.
		- Once a connection has been made, they can project their voice from an extremely distant location into a target’s ears, causing the target to inexplicably die suddenly in a safe location.
		- Similarly, if the **Blatherer** foresees the source of danger (for example via **Malice Perception**), they have the opportunity to exert a countering influence on the spot.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, "They" can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 3 Blatherer owns an Authority.

- [[Corruption (Abyss)]] → the Blatherer's named **Corruption** authority, explicitly concentrated in the Mind and Spirit, matching the ability's effect on a target's sanity and control.
- [[Curses (Abyss)]] → the same ability's power to "hex... with all sorts of Curses" draws on this Authority as a secondary effect.
- The **Mythical Creature Form**'s prayer-response trait names no new power to map.
