# Sequence 8 - Unwinged Angel (Coldblooded)

## Extracted Ability Data

**Description**

Becoming an **Unwinged Angel** means that from that moment forth, they lose their conscience and indulge in their evil desires, possessing bodies that look even more inhuman. They don't have the concept of good and evil anymore.

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their bodies are further strengthened and more inhuman.
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: An **Unwinged Angel** can remain extremely calm and almost emotionless, even during a fierce battle.

**New Abilities**

- **Demonic Attribute**: Upon entering this **Sequence**, they will undergo inhuman changes, acquiring two or three **Devil** **Spell** -like abilities in the process that vary from person to person.
	- Some could wield and create poisonous flames, cast weak **Curses** with **Language of Foulness**, or create a toxic black smoke. The most common of these **Spell** -like abilities is **Slowness**.
		- By raising their arms then abruptly pressing both of their palms down, they can make their target sink, slowing their movements, as though the wings they used for flying had been snapped.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 8 Unwinged Angel owns an Authority.

- [[Deviants (Abyss)]] → **Demonic Attribute** — the "inhuman changes" and person-to-person variation match Deviants' authority over mutation.
- [[Filth (Abyss)]] → the same ability's toxic black smoke and poisonous flames draw on Filth.
- [[Curses (Abyss)]] → the same ability's weak Curses cast "with Language of Foulness" draw on Curses.
- **Physical Enhancement** and **Enhanced Mental Attributes** remain generic traits without a supported Authority assignment.
