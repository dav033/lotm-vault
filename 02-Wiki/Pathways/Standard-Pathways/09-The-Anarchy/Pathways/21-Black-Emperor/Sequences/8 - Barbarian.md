# Sequence 8 - Barbarian

## Extracted Ability Data

**Description**

Problems that cannot be solved by the law will be solved by force. This is also one of the rules.

**Traits  **

- **Physical Enhancement**: Upon advancement, a **Barbarian** will gain various physical improvements such as a physical strength and constitution that breaks the "rules" of a normal human body.
- **Mental Resistance**: In addition to their **Physical Enhancement**, upon advancement, a **Barbarian** will also possess a high resistance to psychological influences.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 8 Barbarian owns an Authority.

- **Physical Enhancement** and **Mental Resistance** remain generic Beyonder traits without a supported Authority assignment; the entry description's framing of force as "one of the rules" is thematic flavor for the pathway's Order-adjacent premise, not an Authority link in itself.
