# Sequence 1 - Prince of Abolition (Abolition)

## Extracted Ability Data

**Authorities**

- **Distortion** & **Definition**: "They" have partial authorities over **Distortion** and **Definition**.
	- A **Prince of Abolition** is able to re- **Define** and **Distort** the existing **Order** to "Their" advantage in order to achieve "Their" desired goals.
		- For example, "They" can **Define** what counts as a **Substitute**, what is the "original body", what counts as **Bribery**, who is "corrupted", how to instantly transform the original body into a **Substitute**, how to turn normal behavior into **Bribery**, and so on.
			- In the midst of "His" silence, [George Augustus III](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/George_Augustus_III "George Augustus III") silently swapped "Himself" with a **Substitute**, which was the **Imagined** version of "Him" doing the speech.
		- "Their" **Distortion** directly **Distorts** the corresponding **Order** and **Rules** (not the underlying ones).
		- With great difficulty, [George Augustus III](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/George_Augustus_III "George Augustus III") split off some of “His” strength, and with the help of the preparations "He" had put in place, "He" forcefully **Distorted** the surrounding area and isolated the dark and solemn secret mausoleum from the real world, preventing earthquakes and meteors from approaching the target.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 1 Prince of Abolition owns an Authority.

- [[Distortion (Black Emperor)]] → **Distortion** — direct partial-authority expression, re-Distorting existing **Order** and **Rules** to the Prince's advantage.
- [[Autocrat (Black Emperor)]] → **Definition** — Definition is a named subordinate authority under **Autocrat** (per the Sequence 0 authority tree), so this reads as a probable partial expression rather than a standalone Authority.
