# Sequence 9 - Arbiter

## Extracted Ability Data

**Traits**

- **Order**: An **Arbiter** will subconsciously maintain the current **Order**, unwilling to disrupt its fabric.
	- If they bear [official identities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Orthodox_Organizations "List of Orthodox Organizations"), this tendency only intensifies.
- **Physical Enhancement**: They have outstanding combat skills to deal with the unexpected.

**New Abilities**

- **Authority**: **Arbiters** possess a convincing charm and have a considerable **Authority**, causing people to be more likely to believe and obey their commands and words.
	- When in conflict against an **Arbiter**, their enemies are often less confident about their chances and will have the feeling of wanting to give up.
