# Sequence 7 - Interrogator

## Extracted Ability Data

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their bodies improve to a certain extent.

**New Abilities**

- **Illusory Torture Devices**: They can summon **Illusory Torture Devices** (like branding irons) in their hands which can directly damage the **Spirit Body**; however, there must be physical contact as these devices cannot be used from a distance.
	- [Xio Derecha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") used an illusory branding iron to stop an [Ancient Wraith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Ancient_Wraith "Creatures and Plants") from attacking her and [Fors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall").
- **Psychic Lashing**: They can coat their weapons in bolts of illusory lightning which lets them turn regular cold weapons into things capable of affecting **Spirit Body** -type creatures.
	- [Xio Derecha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") used **Psychic Lashing** alongside her blade to attack an [Ancient Wraith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Ancient_Wraith "Creatures and Plants").
- **Psychic Piercing**: This **Beyonder** power causes bolts of lightning appear within and shoot out from their eyes to attack their target at an effective distance of *5 meters.*
	- It can impose pressure on the **Spirit Body**, focus the mind, ignore the physical body, pierce the target's **Soul**, and directly attack their **Spirit Body**, bringing unbearable pain.
		- **Psychic Piercing** isn't a **Beyonder** power that disrupted with thoughts or affected the mind, but is a means to directly attack the **Spirit Body**. In essence, while other abilities act on the **Body of Heart and Mind,** **Psychic Piercing** ’s target is the **Soul Body** instead, the two are significantly different.
- **Whip of Pain**: This directly attacks the mind, making the target feel like an electric current are running through their mind, forming a barbed whip that constantly lashes their **Soul**.
	- This "feeling" is both painful and numb, as if it comes from the depths of the brain, making it irresistible. The affected target can only tremble, their knees going weak, and they have to confess as well as dare not lie.
- **Demolition Proficiency**: They have also become demolition experts.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Weapon Proficiency**: They are proficient in using a variety of weapons.
