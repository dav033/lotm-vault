# Sequence 9 - Reader

## Explicit Powers

**Traits**

- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: They gain an enhanced memory, comprehension, and learning abilities.
- **Knowledge Addiction:** They have an unusual desire for **knowledge**.
	- One of the main ingredient for **Reader** is Knowledge Addiction Root, one of the function being to make people addicted to learning knowledge.

**New Abilities**

- **Reading**: They gain a wealth of **Knowledge** through extensive **Reading**.
- **Ritualistic Magic**: They gain the ability to use [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic").

## Authority → Power Mapping

- No Authority is named at Sequence 9. **Reading** and **Knowledge Addiction** are early expressions of the knowledge domain that becomes [[Omniscience (White Tower)]] once the Pathway names its Authorities.
- **Ritualistic Magic** is a general Beyonder technique, not a Pathway-specific Authority; **Enhanced Mental Attributes** remains an unattributed supporting trait.
