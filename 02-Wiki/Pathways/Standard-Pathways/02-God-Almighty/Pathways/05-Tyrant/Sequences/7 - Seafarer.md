# Sequence 7 - Seafarer (Storm Priest)

## Explicit Powers

**Traits**

- **Aquatic Affinity**: **Seafarers** are even more beloved by the oceans and gain an overall enhancement to every aspect of themselves when at sea.
	- They can act and move freely underwater for more than half an hour.
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: Their calculation skills are improved.
	- Having spent the night in the area before, [Alger Wilson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") calculated that the probability of encountering **Beyonder** creatures at the **Demigod** level depended mostly on **Luck**, and that the chances of him and [Gehrman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") encountering one again were not too high.
- **Enhanced Memory**: **Seafarers** possess enhanced memory.

**New Abilities**

- **Navigation**: They are scholars of astronomy and geography. They have an intuitive grasp of magnetic fields, ocean currents, wind direction, and clouds, while also possessing excellent sailing capabilities.
	- They can accurately judge distances, allowing them to precisely throw weapons at a target with their eyes closed after determining the target's location.
		- A boat with a **Seafarer** on board will never get lost at sea due to their newfound grasp of, and affinity with, the fields of **Navigation**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Aquatic Affinity** continues to anticipate [[Water (Tyrant)]]; no Authority is named at this Sequence.
- **Navigation** persists as a named ability through Sequence 3 ([[Sea King|Sea King's]] "A Sea King can never get lost") without ever being called out as its own Authority; it remains an unattributed supporting ability.
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes** and **Enhanced Memory** remain unattributed supporting traits.
