# Sequence 6 - Wind-blessed

## Explicit Powers

**Description**

They are irascible at times, matching the characteristics of a gale. They possess great **Beyonder** powers in domains related to **Water**, **Wind**, and the **Weather**.

**New Abilities**

- **Wind Control**: Blessed by the **Wind**, a **Wind-blessed** can unleash gales from within their own body to create pressure waves or surround themselves with howling **Hurricanes**.
	- **Glide**: They can use the **Winds** to **Glide** through the air if **Flight** has been **Prohibited** by a **[Judge](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Judge "Justiciar Pathway/Abilities")**.
		- A **Wind-blessed** can generate **Wind** beneath their feet to more than double their running speed.
		- **Windblades**: They can create howling, sharp, invisible **Windblades** to slice targets from afar.
		- **Flight** (**Short-distance Flight**): They can **Fly** for short durations and distances.
		- They can imbue their hands with spiraling **Winds** to increase their penetrative power.
		- They can cause objects to be swept up by a gust of **Wind** and fly into their palms.
		- They can **Eavesdrop** on people's hidden conversations using the **Wind**.
		- They can create air cushions to reduce damage taken from impact.
		- **Floating Wind**: A **Wind-blessed** is able to hover in midair.
- **Water Control**: They can manipulate and use **Water** to a certain degree.
- **Dive**: A **Wind-blessed** can **Dive** to a depth of *100 meters*.
- **Float**: They can **Float** on the **Water** without sinking.
- **Night Vision**: They can see in the dark.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Wind Control** (and its Glide, Windblade, short-distance Flight, and Floating Wind expressions) is the direct precursor named ability of [[Wind (Tyrant)]], the Authority Sequence 0 confirms intersects with [[Calamity (Tyrant)]].
- **Water Control**, **Dive**, and **Float** are precursor expressions of [[Water (Tyrant)]].
- **Night Vision** remains an unattributed supporting trait.
